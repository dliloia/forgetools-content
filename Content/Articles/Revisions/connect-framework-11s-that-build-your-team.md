# A Practical 1:1 Agenda for Managers: The CONNECT Framework

A useful one-on-one gives an employee time to think through their work, raise concerns, and discuss where they want to grow. If the meeting is mostly your status questions, you are using their time to get updates you could gather another way.

The CONNECT framework gives a 30-minute meeting a simple shape: ten minutes for personal connection, ten for the employee's work, and ten for growth. The name is a reminder of the purpose: Connect, understand what they are working On, Navigate obstacles, develop their Next skills, Explore goals, Commit to action, and Track follow-through. Use it as a guide, not a script.

## First ten minutes: connect

Start with the person, not the project plan. Ask about something they mentioned last week, how the workload feels, or what is energizing or draining them. Do not treat personal questions as a checklist. Listen, and let the employee decide how much to share.

## Next ten minutes: their work

Let the employee set the first topics. Ask what is moving, what is stuck, and what they want help thinking through. Resist turning every issue into advice. Ask a follow-up question before offering a solution.

If you have topics of your own, make room for them after the employee has raised theirs. A one-on-one is not a substitute for a project review.

## Last ten minutes: growth and commitments

Discuss a skill they want to build, a responsibility they want to try, or a longer-term goal. Development does not require a grand plan every week. It does require returning to the subject often enough that urgent work does not crowd it out.

End by naming one next step, such as an introduction, a decision you owe them, or a follow-up question. Write it down and check it next time.

## Adjust the format

Some weeks need more time for a project risk. Others may focus on a development goal. Keep the purpose steady and adjust the agenda. The structure helps you remember the parts. The relationship depends on how you show up, listen, and follow through.
